Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026 · Version 2.0: playtime, Zen breaks, missions, invites and Discord bot

This is a courtesy translation. If there is any conflict, the Spanish version prevails.

In short: Zeni works without an account and, without one, your games and settings never leave your PC. If you create an account, we store only what's needed to sync your data and show your rank and streams. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads and you can ask us to delete everything whenever you want.

1. Who we are

ZeniPlatform ("Zeni", "we") is a game launcher for Windows developed by an independent team in Chile. This policy explains what data is processed by the desktop app, the Zeni server and this website. For any questions about your data, write to us at contacto@zeni-platform.cl.

2. What data stays on your PC

Without an account, all of this is stored only on your computer and we don't receive it:

  • The list of installed games that Zeni detects (Steam, Epic, Riot, Xbox).
  • Your settings, favorites, recent games, colors and theme.
  • The ranked results you enter by hand.
  • Your playtime per game and per day (see below for how it's measured).
  • How many times you open each game from Zeni (last 30 days), the matchups you open on LoLalytics and your LoL ranked games from the last 12 months: these are used to calculate missions, achievements and animated frames.
  • The weekly recap (Zeni Wrapped): it's created on your PC, and the image only leaves it if you copy or save it to share.

How playtime is measured

While Zeni is open, every 30 seconds it checks the list of programs running on Windows and compares it with the install folders of the games in your library. It only records time for those games: it doesn't look at window contents, doesn't read your files or game memory and doesn't log other programs. The time is stored only on your PC.

Cleaning up the desktop

If you use "Clean up your desktop", Zeni reads your desktop shortcuts to find games and, only if you confirm, moves them to the Documents\Accesos directos (Zeni) folder. It keeps a note of what it moved so it can put them back in one click. None of this leaves your PC.

If you sign in, the session is stored in Windows Credential Manager on your PC.

3. What data we store on the server

Only if you create a Zeni account (it's optional) or use the features that say so:

DataWhat for
Email and display nameTo identify your account and sign you in.
PasswordTo sign you in. We never store your password: only an irreversible hash (Argon2id).
SessionsTo keep you signed in. We store a hash of the session, its expiry date (30 days) and the type of application you signed in from.
Synced dataTo keep your Zeni the same on any PC: your settings (including your Riot ID and server, daily limit, stop-loss and Valorant rank), favorites, colors, theme, profile frame, title and avatar (or the photo from a linked account, if you choose to use it) and your manually entered ranked history (game, win or loss, and date).
Linked accountsIf you link Twitch, Kick, Discord or YouTube: your ID, username, display name and profile picture on that platform. For Twitch and YouTube, also the encrypted access that lets us show who's live.
Kick channelsThe channels you add in the "Live" section, to show you when they're streaming.
Zen breaks and restsEach Zen break or rest you take: the type, date, game and estimated LP saved. Used for your "saved LP" counter, your achievements and your level on any PC.
Claimed missionsWhich mission you claimed (for example "play for 1 hour today"), the XP it gave and the date. The server doesn't receive your playtime: only that you completed the mission.
InvitesYour invite code and, if someone signs up with it, who invited whom (to give the rewards). Whoever invited you sees your display name; you see theirs.
Discord botOnly if you use the /zeni commands: the Discord servers you joined with /zeni unirme and, for each server, the channel its admins chose for notices.
FeedbackIf you use "Send feedback": the message, its type, the Zeni version, your account (if you were signed in) and a contact email only if you leave one.

Permissions we request when linking accounts

  • Twitch: to see the channels you follow, so we can show you who's live. For this we store the access granted by Twitch, encrypted (AES-256-GCM).
  • YouTube (Google): read-only access to your profile, your channel and the list of channels you subscribe to, so we can show you which ones are live. For this we store the access granted by Google, encrypted (AES-256-GCM). We don't post anything, don't change your subscriptions and don't read your history or comments.
  • Kick and Discord: only to read your basic profile. We don't store access to those accounts.

We never see your password for those platforms: authorization happens on their own page. When you unlink an account, we delete its data and revoke the access with the platform. You can also remove Zeni's access directly from each platform; on Google, from your account's security settings.

YouTube and Google data

Zeni uses the YouTube API Services. When you link YouTube, the Google Privacy Policy also applies. We use your subscriptions only to show you which channels are live: we keep them in memory for a few hours and don't share them with anyone.

ZeniPlatform's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The Zeni Discord bot

The bot is optional and only works with your Discord linked to Zeni. When you type /zeni unirme in a server, you agree that the members of that server can see, in that server:

  • Your Zeni display name and your League of Legends rank (in /zeni rango and /zeni ranking).
  • A daily recap of your ranked wins and losses for that day (LoL and what you enter for Valorant).
  • A notice when you hit your stop-loss, only if you turned on the option to notify your servers in the app's Settings.

You can stop sharing at any time with /zeni salir in each server or by turning off the notice in Settings. What the bot has already posted stays on Discord, under that server's rules and the Discord Privacy Policy.

Your League of Legends rank

To show your rank and today's games, our server queries the official Riot Games API with your Riot ID and server. The response is sent to your app (or to the Discord server where it was requested with the bot) and we don't store it in the database; it only stays in memory for a few minutes to avoid repeating requests.

Technical data

Like any internet service, our server receives your IP address. We use it only to limit abuse (for example, too many sign-in attempts) and don't store it in the database. Our server provider may keep technical logs for a limited time.

4. What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your data.
  • We don't show ads or use your data for advertising profiles.
  • We don't read your files, matches or game memory. For playtime, we only see which games in your library are open.
  • This website doesn't use tracking cookies.

5. Who we share data with

Only with the services needed for Zeni to work:

  • Railway (United States): hosts our server and database.
  • Vercel: hosts this website.
  • GitHub: hosts the installers and updates.
  • Discord: we receive the feedback you send us there, in a private team channel. If you use the bot, it posts what's described above in the servers you joined.
  • Riot Games, Twitch, Kick, Discord and Google (YouTube): only when you use the related feature (rank, "Live" or linking accounts), and only what that feature needs.

If you turn on "Show my activity on Discord", Zeni shares what you're doing in Zeni (for example, which game you're choosing) with the Discord app on your PC. You can turn it off in Settings.

6. How long we keep data

  • Your account data, for as long as you have the account.
  • Sessions expire after 30 days or when you sign out.
  • Linked accounts, until you unlink them or delete your account.
  • Zen breaks, claimed missions and invites, for as long as you have the account.
  • Your participation in a Discord server, until you use /zeni salir or delete your account.
  • Feedback, for as long as it's useful for improving Zeni.

7. Your rights

You can delete your account yourself at any time from the app: open your profile and click Delete my account (Eliminar mi cuenta). This instantly deletes your account, your synced data, your linked accounts (revoking Zeni's access on each platform), your Kick channels, your Zen breaks, your claimed missions, your invites, your participation in Discord servers and the feedback you sent. We don't receive what's stored only on your PC (playtime, games, settings): you delete it by uninstalling Zeni and selecting the option to remove the application data.

You can also ask us at any time to access, correct or delete your data. Write to contacto@zeni-platform.cl from your account's email and we'll reply within 15 business days. You can also unlink your accounts and sign out directly in the app.

We process your data in accordance with Chilean personal data protection law.

8. Security

All communication is encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords and sessions are stored as irreversible hashes, Twitch and YouTube access is encrypted, Discord bot messages are verified with Discord's signature and app updates are signed, so that only official versions of Zeni get installed. No system is 100% secure, but we do what's reasonable to protect your data and will let you know if a problem affects you.

9. Minors

Zeni is intended for people aged 13 or older, the same minimum age required by Riot Games, Twitch and Discord. If you are a minor, use Zeni with the permission of a parent or guardian.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. If the change is important, we'll announce it in the app or on this site.
