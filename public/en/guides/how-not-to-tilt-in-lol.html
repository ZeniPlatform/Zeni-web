How not to tilt in LoL: stop-loss and ranked limits

Guide · 6-minute read

We've all been there: you lose a ranked game because your jungler never showed up, you queue again "to win it back", you lose another… and at 3 a.m. you're on five straight losses and down 80 LP. That's tilt, and it's the fastest way to drop a league. The good news: a couple of simple rules can prevent it.

What tilt is in League of Legends

Tilt is when frustration starts making your decisions. You play more aggressively than you should, blame your team, stop looking at the minimap and take fights you shouldn't. Worst of all, when you're tilted, you feel sure you'll win the next game, and that's why you keep playing.

Signs that you're tilted

  • You type in chat (or want to) to blame someone.
  • You play "one more to win the LP back" right after losing.
  • You get angry before champion select is over.
  • You play champions outside your pool "to try something".
  • You can't remember why you lost your last games: only that it was someone else's fault.

If two or more sound familiar, it's time to stop.

The stop-loss rule

The stop-loss is an idea from investing: you decide beforehand how much you're willing to lose, and when you reach that point, you get out, no arguments. It works the same way in LoL:

Rule: if you lose 2 ranked games in a row, stop. Take a break of at least 20 minutes before playing ranked again (or call it a day).

Why 2? Because one loss can be bad luck, but two in a row often mean something has changed in you: tiredness, frustration or a lack of focus. If you play more calmly, you can raise it to 3. What matters is deciding before you start, not in the heat of the moment.

How many ranked games should you play per day?

There's no magic number, but there is a clear idea: quality drops with every game. After several hours, your reaction time and decisions get worse, even if you don't notice. A good starting point:

  • 3 to 5 ranked games a day if you want to climb steadily.
  • Play your most important games when you're rested, not late at night.
  • If you reach your limit while winning, stop anyway: you'll play better tomorrow.

Habits that help you avoid tilt

  • Mute chat from the start (/mute all). It saves you arguments that won't change the outcome.
  • Take a short break between games: get up, drink some water, don't queue again right away.
  • Look at why you lost, not whose fault it was. Review one of your own plays that you could have done better.
  • Play normals or ARAM if you want to keep playing after your stop-loss.
  • Get enough sleep. It sounds obvious, but it's what affects your level the most.
