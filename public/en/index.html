All your games, in one place

What Zeni does with any game on your PC.

Your library

No more digging for games in a thousand folders

Zeni automatically finds the games you have installed on Steam, Epic Games, Riot and Xbox, and arranges them in a library with cover art. No more opening four different launchers or filling your desktop with shortcuts.

  • Detects your games instantly, with nothing to set up.
  • Search any game by typing its name (shortcut /).
  • Favorites, recently played and a page for each game with screenshots.
  • Real playtime: today, this week and in total, measured only while the game is open.
Missions and levels New

Play what you love and level up

Daily, weekly, monthly and yearly missions for any game: play for 1 hour today, try 2 new games this month, play on 150 days this year. The long ones give thousands of XP.

  • Everyone gets the same missions each day: compete with your friends.
  • More than 20 achievements, for 100 hours played or 100 missions claimed.
  • Unlock avatars, titles, colors, animated backgrounds and fire, galaxy and rainbow frames for your profile.
Zeni Wrapped New

Your gaming week in one image

Every Monday, Zeni puts together your recap: hours played, your top games, your best day, ranked, saved LP and XP earned. Copy it and share it on Discord, WhatsApp or social media.

Clean desktop New

A clean desktop in one click

Zeni finds the game shortcuts scattered across your desktop, adds them to your library and stores them in a folder. Even those from launchers it doesn't detect on its own, like Battle.net or Minecraft.

  • You choose which ones to move.
  • Nothing gets deleted: you can put them back on your desktop anytime.
Live

Twitch and Kick without opening a browser

Link your accounts and see which streamers you follow are live. Click one and the stream opens inside Zeni, alongside OP.GG, U.GG, LoLalytics and Tracker.gg.

And when you play ranked

Extra features for people who compete in League of Legends and Valorant.

Stop-loss

"Just one more"… not anymore.

Zeni counts today's games. If you lose several in a row, it warns you before you open another ranked game and suggests a break. It's still your call, but now you'll think twice.

  • Configurable stop-loss: you choose after how many losses.
  • Daily ranked limit.
  • Works with League of Legends and Valorant.
  • Zen Mode: one minute of guided breathing to cool your head.
  • A saved LP counter for every time you stop in time.

Live rank

Your rank and LP always at a glance

Enter your Riot ID once and Zeni shows your rank, your LP and today's games, with champion and KDA, right in your library.

Matchups New

Your matchup in 2 seconds

Open LoLalytics inside Zeni, type "ahri vs zed mid" in the top bar and press Enter: Zeni takes you to the exact matchup page, with build, runes and lane. No Google, no tabs.

  • Understands nicknames: mf, j4, cait, yi…
  • Always up to date with new champions.

Frequently asked questions

Is ZeniPlatform free?

Yes, and it always will be for players. No ads, and we don't sell your data. Zeni Analytics and Zeni Business are separate products and don't have pricing yet.

Is the app in English?

Not yet: the Zeni app is currently available in Spanish only. This website is available in both languages.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

Yes. It appears because Zeni is a new app and doesn't have a Windows signing certificate yet (they are very expensive for an independent project). Click More info → Run anyway. Updates are signed: Zeni only installs official versions.

Do I need an account?

No. The library, the stop-loss and the ranked limit work without one. A Zeni account is optional and lets you see your LoL rank, sync across PCs, link Twitch, Kick and Discord, and earn XP with missions and achievements.

Can Riot ban me for using Zeni?

No. Zeni doesn't touch game files or interfere with your matches: it only opens the game and checks your rank with Riot's official API, just like OP.GG.

Do missions work with any game?

Yes. Missions use what Zeni measures in any game in your library: playtime, days played, different games and new games. To claim the XP you need a free Zeni account.

How does it update?

By itself. When a new version comes out, Zeni lets you know and installs it in one click, without losing your data.

I found a bug or have an idea

Tell us! Inside Zeni, in the sidebar, click Send feedback (Enviar comentarios). We read every message.
